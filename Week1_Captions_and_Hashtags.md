**Days 1–7 · Mon 19 Oct → Sun 25 Oct 2026 · South Africa · savings-proof model**

Everything below is copy-paste ready **except** the items in each day's *Swap before posting* box. Those are placeholders for proof only you hold — a caption with someone else's numbers in it is a misrepresentation, even if the format is perfect.

**How discovery works now:** hashtags are a rounding error. Searchable *keywords inside the caption* are what surface you in Instagram and Facebook search, so every caption below is written to contain its keywords naturally in the first two lines. Hashtags stay as a small rotated set for category signalling — nothing more.

# The Hashtag System

### Rotation sets — never repeat a set on consecutive posts
| Set | Use on | Tags |
|---|---|---|
| **A — proof & rates** | rate comparisons, booking proof | #WholesaleTravel #TravelSouthAfrica #FamilyHoliday #HotelDeals #SAFamilyTravel #TravelDeals |
| **B — education & tips** | explainers, checklists, myth-busting | #TravelTips #HolidayPlanning #BookingTips #TravelSmart #FamilyTravel #LocalIsLekker |
| **C — seasonal & geo** | destination spotlights, customer stories | #DecemberHoliday #SchoolHolidaysSA #ZanzibarHoliday #MauritiusHoliday #CapeTown #Drakensberg #MozambiqueTravel |
| **D — Lane B, neutral** | partner-side posts only | #TravelBusiness #SmallBusinessSA #SideProjectSA |

### Rules
1. **Five to eight tags, never thirty.** Large dead tags (#travel, #love, #blessed) add reach from people who will never book a family holiday in Mauritius.
2. **Rotate A → B → C** across the week so the same block never appears twice in seven days.
3. **Instagram:** tags in the first comment, not the caption — keeps the caption's keyword lines clean. **Facebook:** skip tags or use three; they do little there.
4. **Geo-tags the location sticker instead:** tag the resort or city on Stories; that's local discovery that hashtags can't touch.

### Never use — on any post, either lane
`#MLM` `#NetworkMarketing` `#PassiveIncome` `#ResidualIncome` `#SideHustle` `#WorkFromHome` `#BusinessOpportunity` `#FinancialFreedom` `#GetRich` `#BossBabe` `#Hustle`

Two reasons: they pull an audience looking for income schemes (exactly the suspicion you're working against), and they're the tags platform risk systems associate with deceptive earnings claims. Your travel proof content should look, to every classifier and every sceptic, like travel content.

# Caption Keyword Bank
Weave two or three of these into the first two lines of any caption you write yourself — this is what search actually reads:

- wholesale hotel rates South Africa
- contracted hotel rates explained
- cheaper hotel rates Mauritius / Zanzibar / Mozambique
- December school holidays travel South Africa
- family holiday budget South Africa
- how hotel pricing works
- family of four holiday cost

**The one-line test before any caption goes out:** *if a stranger screenshot this and sent it to the National Consumer Commission, would I be comfortable?* If the answer is anything but an instant yes, the caption isn't finished.
